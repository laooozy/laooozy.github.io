ACG

I shoot as a coser, and I'm an amateur who has only just picked up a camera.

Coser photography

I got my first camera in August 2026. Everything is still a work in progress — composition, light, and talking with the coser are all things I practise a little more each day.

How I shoot

  • We talk first

    Before we shoot I like to chat with the coser about the character and the mood they want, so the photos come closer to what they have in mind.

  • Slow and steady

    I'm in no rush to get to the next scene; I'd rather wait for the right light and the right expression.

  • Still learning

    Honestly, I'm still new — I'll make mistakes and adjust as I go, but I want each shoot to be a little better than the last.

Commissions & collaboration

Where I am right now:

  • No paid private shoots for now.
  • Free between friends.
  • Not really in the scene — just a coser who likes taking photos.
  • I reply to every DM, so feel free to reach out.
