# PRODUCT

**laooozy** — the personal site of a coser-cum-amateur-photographer who also
writes, plus an ACG (anime / comic / games) persona. Fully static Astro on
GitHub Pages; no CMS, no server, no backend.

## Register

**brand** — design _is_ the product. This is a personal identity site, not a
tool. The home page is a masthead: it has to read as a person, not a dashboard.

## Who / where / mood

A coser in Hong Kong, browsing at night on a laptop or phone, lights low. The
site is near-black, lit by a purple aurora, with quiet glass surfaces. It should
feel like stepping into a darkroom: calm, deliberate, a little luminous — never
loud, never busy.

## Users & jobs

1. **A visitor who just met the handle** — wants to know who this is and what
   they do in one screen. Job: understand the person fast.
2. **A potential shoot partner (coser or friend)** — wants to see the ACG side
   and how to reach out. Job: get to `/acg/` and Instagram.
3. **A reader** — wants the latest writing. Job: get to a post in one click.

## Principles

- **One screen tells the story.** The fold carries the wordmark, the person, the
  "now" line, and the way in. No dead air.
- **Composed, not decorated.** Depth comes from surface, light and space, not
  from ornament. No gradient text, no stripes, no sketchy SVG. The deep-space
  atmosphere (nebula, star dust, right-to-left shooting stars, purple
  lightning) is sanctioned: it is light and space, not decoration. Under
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` it goes still: no drift, no stars, no
  lightning, and the entrance reveal is instant.
- **Static-first.** Content and layout are HTML+CSS. JS is reserved for what is
  genuinely interactive or alive: the scroll-hiding bar, Pagefind search, the
  atmospheric canvas, and the entrance reveal.
- **The kit is the product.** Pages are assembled from a small set of composable
  parts (layout primitives + UI + content) so any layout can be arranged freely.

## Voice

Warm, plain, first-person, slightly self-deprecating. Authored in Traditional
Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English — never machine-translated.

## Hard constraints

Dark-only (no light theme). Three locales (`zh-hant` default, `zh-hans`, `en`).
No hosted photos — link out to Instagram. No real name — the handle only.
